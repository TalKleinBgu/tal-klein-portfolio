Introduction

About Me

I am a Machine Learning Research Engineer and Data Scientist who thrives at the intersection of advanced academic research and practical software applications. Having recently completed my M.Sc. in Software and Information Systems Engineering at Ben-Gurion University (as part of the Meitar Excellence Program), I am highly motivated to dive into the industry, with a strong drive to continuously learn, quickly master new technologies, and tackle cutting-edge challenges, and grow into a leading professional in the field.

My core technical focus spans Machine Learning, Data Science, and Advanced AI, with deep-dive research experience in Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Causal Inference. For my Master's thesis, I applied advanced NLP and LLM-based feature extraction to analyze large-scale legal data, using Causal Inference methodologies to detect and measure sentencing disparities.

I’m an easygoing person who genuinely enjoys collaborative problem-solving, open brainstorming, and working with people who are passionate about what they do. I believe that the best technical solutions come from a mix of curiosity, good communication, and a healthy sense of humor.

When I'm not training models, I'm probably watching basketball (big fan of Deni Avdija) or talking about my meaningful experience raising a guide dog puppy.
